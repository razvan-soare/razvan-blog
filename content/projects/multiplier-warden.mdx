The products are private, so there are no screenshots, but here is what I do there. Most of it is frontend in a large TypeScript monorepo, with some backend when a feature needs it. The one public piece is the Multiplier website linked above, a full page scrolling site with an animated logo intro, built with Next.js, Tailwind and Framer Motion.

#### What I did
- Built the interface where a person reviews what an AI model extracted from a document, such as a payslip or a capital gains statement. The PDF sits next to an editable grid, clicking a value highlights where it came from, and a missing value can be marked by drawing on the page.
- Added drill down into nested tables like earnings and deductions, and saving a reviewed document as a golden set, a trusted answer that new versions of the extractor are tested against.
- Built internal tools for the team that works on the extractors, such as a visualizer to browse and edit golden sets.
- Improved the developer workflow in the monorepo, with caching, faster tests and cleaner process handling.

#### Skills I used
Complex interaction design, PDF and document UIs, working with AI output that is sometimes wrong, internal tooling, and developer experience at monorepo scale.
