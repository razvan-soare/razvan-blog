HonorCycles is a bike servicing, shipping and storage service in London. A colleague built the backend and I built the frontend, a flow where people choose their bike and book precisely the repair they need.

<VideoGifImg type="gif" path="honorcycles/select-bike.gif" style={{ width: '70%', margin: '0 auto' }}/>

#### What I did
- Designed and built the booking flow, with custom images for each bike type, so booking takes very little effort.
- Took payment with Stripe.
- Worked in a two person team, with a colleague on the backend.

#### Skills I used
User flow design, shipping a whole product quickly in a small team, and payments.
