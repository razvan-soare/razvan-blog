#### 💻 Technologies used

*Tapnote* is an AI system that learns from dentists' clinical findings to suggest diagnoses and special tests, and produces complete patient records. I worked on its frontend in React and Typescript.

<VideoGifImg type="image" path="aura-dental/aura-ipad-landscape.png"/>

#### What I did
- Built the screens where a dentist compares treatments side by side, with benefits, risks, duration, cost and custom additions, so a patient can give informed consent.
- Worked on how the AI's suggested diagnoses and templates are presented to the dentist.
- Added a guided product tour for new users and the Stripe payment flow.

#### Skills I used
Designing dense, data heavy interfaces for a tablet, working with an AI backed product, onboarding flows and payments.
