#### 💻 Technologies used

<List items={['ReactJs', 'NextJs', 'Typescript', 'GraphQL', 'Prisma', 'AWS AppSync', 'AWS CDK', 'Strapi', 'Turborepo', 'Klaviyo', 'Primer', 'New Relic']} />

*7879* sells fine jewellery in gold, platinum and silver, priced by the weight of each piece and the metal market rate of the day. I was a full stack developer there, mostly on the frontend, working in a monorepo with a Next.js shop, an internal operations app and a Strapi CMS.

<VideoGifImg type="img" path="7879/product.jpg"/>

#### What I did
- Built the page builder, so the team can put together content pages without a developer.
- Built the product listing, where customers filter by metal, gender, size, price and style.
- Built the checkout, including the payment integration, and the product page that breaks a price down into weight, metal value, manufacturing cost, fee and VAT.
- Wrote the backend pieces behind them, GraphQL APIs on AWS.

#### Skills I used
Full stack development end to end, designing flexible content tooling, ecommerce and payments, and working across a large monorepo.
