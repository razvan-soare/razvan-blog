##### [Check live](https://app.bloomd.com/)

#### 💻 Technologies used

<List items={['ReactJs', 'Typescript', 'Styled Components', 'Intersection Observer', 'Axios', 'Moment', 'Iproov','Reactour', 'Talkjs', 'Twilio']} />

*Bloomd* connects older and younger people so they can share knowledge, experience and company. I worked on the frontend as a PWA, so it runs in the browser on any device and can also be installed like a desktop app.

<VideoGifImg type="image" path="bloomd/page1.png"/>

#### What I did
- Built a simple, readable interface designed for older users.
- Made it an installable PWA that works on mobile and desktop.
- Worked on the badge system that rewards people on their profile.
- Integrated Twilio and TalkJS for chat, audio and video appointments between users, plus a guided tour for new users.

#### Skills I used
Accessible design for older users, building PWAs, real time communication, and integrating third party services such as video, chat and identity checks.
