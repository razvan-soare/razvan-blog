Dizinga is a platform where creators upload their art and sell merchandise to their audience. I worked closely with the frontend team to build the artist panel.

<VideoGifImg type="img" path="dizinga/view1.png" />

#### What I did
- Built a responsive interface connected to the backend, showing each artist their own data.
- Built the orders overview, the flow to create a product and add it to the store, and the pages to check and edit earlier designs.

#### Skills I used
Responsive design, connecting a React interface to an API, and building a dashboard for non technical users.
